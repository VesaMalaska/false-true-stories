# False True Stories — World Seed: Maximilian Toranaga

This document contains the canonical starting truth for this Story Pack.

It defines what must be true when a new Story Run begins, what should remain unknown, and where the narrator has creative freedom.

It does not contain events or consequences belonging to any particular Story Run.

## Runtime readiness

**Ready to play** — the World Seed has crossed the creative playability threshold. Environment and persistence readiness must be established separately before runtime launch.

## Core premise

A glamorous espionage adventure set in a heightened version of the real human world.

Behind wealth, diplomacy, advanced technology, elegant social events, and polished public appearances lies another layer of reality: intelligence operations, industrial espionage, hidden agendas, secret research, political influence, surveillance, deception, and people whose real interests are rarely obvious.

At the centre of the story is **Maximilian Kenji Toranaga**, heir to a powerful technology empire and an unconventional independent actor in the intelligence world.

Maximilian is not a hardened professional spy employed by a single service. He is wealthy, highly intelligent, resourceful, technically well connected, socially influential, and increasingly experienced in espionage.

He sometimes cooperates with intelligence services, governments, private organizations, or other actors when their interests align with his own.

At other times, even experienced professionals may be uncertain whose interests he is truly serving.

For Maximilian, espionage is partly serious business, partly personal vocation, partly dangerous hobby, and partly entertainment.

Something deeper also drives him.

That deeper reason remains unknown.

## Creative inspiration

The intended atmosphere draws inspiration from classic **James Bond** espionage fiction:

- glamour;
- international intrigue;
- elegant social environments;
- extravagant technology;
- daring operations;
- colourful adversaries;
- serious danger hidden beneath polished surfaces.

**Quentin Tarantino** is an additional tonal reference for:

- tense dialogue;
- eccentric personalities;
- awkward bravado;
- surprising shifts in social power;
- strange human behaviour under pressure;
- character-driven humour inside genuinely dangerous situations.

These are references to qualities of atmosphere, pacing, humour, and character interaction.

The story must not imitate protected characters, plots, settings, or distinctive expression from those works.

The world is also not tied to a specific historical year.

Its visual and cultural texture may evoke glamorous late-20th-century espionage fiction, but the exact period should remain undefined unless the story later establishes one.

## Protagonist or listener position

The central protagonist is:

**Maximilian Kenji Toranaga**

Common forms of his name carry different degrees of familiarity:

- **Maximilian** — formal and public usage;
- **Max** — ordinary familiar usage;
- **Ken** — primarily family and genuinely close friends.

Maximilian is entirely fictional and should not be treated as a fictionalized version of the listener or creator.

He was born into considerable wealth and belongs to the Toranaga industrial family.

The family business empire began with Maximilian's Japanese grandfather, who moved from Japan to Berlin, married a German woman, and founded **Toranaga Tech**.

The grandfather built the original enterprise through technical ability, precision, determination, and strength of character.

The family enterprise continued through the following generation, and Maximilian has continued his father's life's work.

Today Maximilian owns or controls multiple high-technology companies involved in advanced research and development.

These companies are genuine operating businesses rather than espionage fronts.

They are mature enough and staffed by enough capable executives, engineers, researchers, and specialists that Maximilian no longer needs to spend most of his life handling everyday management.

This gives him unusual amounts of:

- freedom;
- wealth;
- technical access;
- social influence;
- international mobility;
- time.

### Capabilities

Maximilian is highly intelligent and resourceful.

He has trained seriously in:

- Krav Maga;
- karate;
- judo.

He has practical ability but is not an elite master of those disciplines.

Likewise, he is capable in espionage but is not a military-trained super-agent or perfectly disciplined intelligence professional.

His advantages come from a combination of:

- intelligence;
- confidence;
- wealth;
- technical resources;
- social access;
- improvisation;
- curiosity;
- nerve;
- unconventional thinking.

He may have access to expensive equipment, experimental technology, unusual vehicles, or advanced gadgets when circumstances plausibly permit it.

His connection to Toranaga Tech and its research capabilities may sometimes provide access to technology unavailable to ordinary people.

This must not mean that every Toranaga company or employee secretly belongs to an intelligence organization.

### Operational personality

Maximilian is willing to attempt things that trained professionals may consider unnecessarily risky.

A professional operative may look at a situation and see:

- insufficient preparation;
- excessive exposure;
- unreliable odds;
- unnecessary personal danger.

Maximilian may look at the same situation and think:

**It might work.**

His willingness to ignore conventional caution sometimes produces extraordinary results precisely because nobody expected anyone sensible to behave that way.

It can also create genuine trouble.

His wealth and intelligence do not make bad decisions retroactively wise.

## Character flaw: dignity and revenge

Maximilian has an unusually sensitive sense of personal dignity.

This is one of his most important weaknesses.

He does not normally react to humiliation or insult with visible anger.

If somebody embarrasses him, questions his intelligence, makes him look incompetent, or otherwise wounds his pride, he may simply smile, remain composed, and continue the conversation.

Internally, however, he may begin planning revenge almost immediately.

His preferred revenge is usually not an uncontrolled physical response.

He prefers **symmetry and humiliation**.

If someone embarrasses Maximilian publicly, he is strongly tempted to arrange circumstances in which that person is embarrassed even more effectively and, if possible, just as publicly.

Because Maximilian is intelligent, these retaliations may become elaborate.

Because his pride is a weakness, they may also be completely unnecessary.

His desire for revenge can:

- distract him from the real objective;
- generate side missions;
- waste time;
- create enemies;
- complicate otherwise successful operations.

Maximilian is often most dangerous immediately after appearing completely unbothered by an insult.

## Relationship with the intelligence world

Maximilian does not belong wholly to any one intelligence agency.

He may cooperate with:

- state intelligence services;
- governments;
- private actors;
- security organizations;
- independent operatives;
- other interested parties.

These relationships depend on circumstance and overlapping interests.

Cooperation does not automatically establish permanent allegiance.

Different actors may regard Maximilian as:

- an ally;
- a useful independent asset;
- an unpredictable amateur;
- a valuable contact;
- a dangerous nuisance;
- a source of technology or information;
- someone whose loyalties are difficult to determine.

No single interpretation should be universally correct.

His independence must remain meaningful.

### Toranaga Tech and counter-espionage

Toranaga Tech's advanced research has attracted attempts to steal its secrets.

The company has faced espionage directed at its laboratories, researchers, prototypes, and technological work.

Ordinary corporate security eventually proved insufficient.

As a result, Toranaga Tech was forced to develop increasingly sophisticated methods of protecting itself.

Over time, the organization acquired meaningful expertise in **counter-espionage**.

This history provides Maximilian with an organic connection to the intelligence world.

Espionage first came to the Toranaga family because other people wanted what their companies possessed.

Exactly how Maximilian's involvement evolved from protecting family interests into his present independent adventures remains undefined.

## Initial relationships

No mandatory close companion, handler, romantic partner, intelligence contact, rival, or permanent operational team is established at the beginning.

Maximilian has:

- family;
- close friends;
- employees;
- business contacts;
- social acquaintances;
- connections within the intelligence world.

The specific people occupying those roles should emerge naturally through the story unless required by established continuity.

Someone calling him **Ken** should normally indicate genuine personal closeness or an established family connection rather than casual familiarity.

Future:

- loyalties;
- friendships;
- rivalries;
- romances;
- betrayals;
- recurring professional relationships

remain open to development during play.

## Reality and boundaries

The world is a **heightened version of recognizable human reality**.

It is not fantasy.

It is not a superhero reality.

It is not unrestricted science fiction.

Human beings remain human.

Ordinary physical reality remains meaningful.

Physics should remain recognizable.

Weapons can kill.

Falls can kill.

Violence can cause injury.

Technology requires some plausible physical basis even when it is unusually advanced or extravagant.

Money and technology provide major advantages but do not remove physical consequences.

Within those boundaries, the world may stretch plausibility enough to support glamorous espionage adventure.

It may contain:

- extravagant but plausible gadgets;
- unusual experimental technology;
- daring operations;
- elaborate security systems;
- ambitious conspiracies;
- eccentric adversaries;
- spectacular locations;
- unusually large or theatrical schemes.

The world may be colourful and heightened without becoming cartoonish.

## World atmosphere

The visible world is polished.

Money, reputation, diplomacy, business, politics, technology, status, and social influence matter.

Important conversations may happen at elegant private gatherings while entirely different negotiations occur beneath the surface.

People may attend the same event for completely different reasons.

An apparently harmless social encounter may actually involve:

- surveillance;
- recruitment;
- intelligence gathering;
- bribery;
- seduction;
- deception;
- manipulation;
- information exchange.

The atmosphere should often contain two simultaneous realities:

**what everyone appears to be doing**

and

**what several of them are actually doing.**

Glamour must not remove humanity.

Powerful and dangerous people can still be:

- petty;
- vain;
- strange;
- charming;
- insecure;
- funny;
- pompous;
- awkward;
- foolish;
- charismatic.

## Tone and danger

The primary tone is:

- adventurous;
- glamorous;
- suspenseful;
- playful;
- intelligent;
- occasionally dark;
- genuinely dangerous;
- frequently touched by humour.

Humour must not invalidate danger.

Serious situations may become unexpectedly funny.

Funny situations may suddenly become dangerous.

Failure is possible.

People may be injured.

Plans may collapse.

Operations may have lasting consequences.

Important characters may die.

Death is genuinely possible within the world.

### Protagonist survival

For the sake of an ongoing adventure, the story may give Maximilian a strong degree of narrative survival preference, especially during the early story.

This must not function as obvious supernatural protection or guaranteed immunity.

Maximilian may occasionally survive catastrophically bad situations because of:

- unexpected ingenuity;
- coincidence;
- another person's mistake;
- audacity;
- a ridiculous chain of events;
- sheer good fortune.

Sometimes he may emerge from circumstances that appeared certain to injure or kill him with almost no damage at all.

These escapes may contribute to the story's humour.

They must remain surprising.

Maximilian must never be able to rely on recklessness always succeeding.

Sometimes the balcony really is a terrible idea.

## Opening situation

A new Story Run begins at a **private party hosted at the Polish ambassador's manor**.

The exact city, country, diplomatic posting, and historical date are not predetermined.

The ambassador has invited wealthy, politically influential, socially important, and otherwise useful people from the surrounding elite.

The party is prestigious, expensive, and deliberately impressive.

Its public purpose is social.

Its less public purpose is also clear to anyone familiar with political society:

the ambassador intends the evening to strengthen his influence, network, reputation, and political standing.

### Maximilian's position at the party

Maximilian Toranaga is one of the evening's most important guests.

He has been a major supporter and sponsor of the event.

His wealth, reputation, business interests, and contribution to the evening give him an entirely legitimate reason to attend and unusually favourable access to the ambassador and the manor.

To most observers, Maximilian is simply behaving as the wealthy, socially active industrialist they expect him to be.

He has another reason for being there.

Maximilian deliberately wants to get close to the ambassador.

He believes that somewhere inside the ambassador's private office or working room are **rare and highly classified documents**.

He considers the office safe the most likely place in which those documents are hidden.

The party is already underway when the story begins.

The runtime should enter the event itself rather than explaining Maximilian's entire biography before the first scene.

## Canonical starting truths

At the beginning of every new Story Run created from this seed:

1. Maximilian Kenji Toranaga is the central protagonist.
2. Maximilian is a wealthy industrial heir with Japanese and German family roots.
3. His Japanese grandfather moved to Berlin, married a German woman, and founded Toranaga Tech.
4. The Toranaga family business empire continued through Maximilian's father and has been continued further by Maximilian.
5. Maximilian owns or controls multiple high-technology and research-oriented companies.
6. Those companies are established enough to operate largely through hired specialists and professional leadership, leaving Maximilian considerable personal freedom.
7. Toranaga Tech has historically been targeted by people attempting to steal valuable research and technological secrets.
8. Those threats forced Toranaga Tech to develop meaningful expertise in counter-espionage.
9. Maximilian's connection to espionage grew organically from this environment, but the complete history of that development is not yet established.
10. Maximilian is intelligent, resourceful, wealthy, socially connected, technologically well supplied, and unusually willing to take risks.
11. He has meaningful training in Krav Maga, karate, and judo but is not an elite master of those disciplines.
12. He is not a hardened professional intelligence operative or military-trained super-agent.
13. His unconventional behaviour and willingness to attempt things professionals consider unwise are central characteristics.
14. Maximilian has a strong sensitivity concerning his dignity and reputation.
15. He normally hides anger or embarrassment rather than displaying it openly.
16. When publicly humiliated, he is strongly inclined to plan a more effective public humiliation in return.
17. This desire for revenge can distract him from important objectives and create unnecessary complications.
18. Maximilian does not belong completely to one intelligence service.
19. He may cooperate with different intelligence organizations or other actors according to circumstance and overlapping interests.
20. Other participants in the intelligence world may not always know whose side Maximilian is on.
21. Espionage is partly serious business for Maximilian and partly something he finds exciting and entertaining.
22. A deeper personal reason also drives his involvement, but that reason is not yet known.
23. The world is a heightened but recognizably human reality.
24. Glamour, extravagant espionage technology, eccentric personalities, ambitious schemes, and remarkable situations are allowed.
25. Physical danger remains real and consequential.
26. Maximilian is narratively favoured to survive, especially early in the story, but he possesses no supernatural immunity.
27. The opening takes place at a private party at the Polish ambassador's manor.
28. Maximilian is an important sponsor and special guest at that party.
29. He has a private reason for getting close to the ambassador.
30. Maximilian believes highly classified documents are hidden somewhere in the ambassador's private office or working room, probably inside a safe.

## Character knowledge and beliefs

At the beginning:

- Maximilian knows why he personally wants access to the ambassador and the documents, even though that reason does not need to be predetermined before it becomes narratively relevant.
- Maximilian believes the classified documents exist.
- Maximilian believes they are located somewhere in the ambassador's private office or working room.
- Maximilian suspects the safe is the most likely location.
- These beliefs are not automatically equivalent to objective world truth.
- Other characters know only what their own circumstances plausibly allow them to know.
- Nobody should recognize Maximilian as a secret intelligence actor merely because the narrator knows that aspect of his life.
- His public reputation as a wealthy industrialist and socially active member of elite circles provides genuine camouflage.

## Intentionally unknown

The following questions are deliberately reserved for discovery:

- What exactly are the classified documents?
- Why does Maximilian want them?
- How did Maximilian learn or come to believe that they exist?
- Are the documents actually in the ambassador's office?
- Are they actually inside the safe?
- Is Maximilian's information completely correct?
- Does anyone else at the party want the same documents?
- What other hidden agendas are operating during the party?
- What is the ambassador actually involved in, if anything?
- What is Maximilian's deeper personal reason for pursuing espionage?
- What happened during the early espionage attacks against Toranaga Tech?
- How exactly did Toranaga Tech develop its counter-espionage capabilities?
- What role did Maximilian's father or grandfather play in that development?
- What event or sequence of events drew Maximilian personally into active espionage?
- Which intelligence organizations know him?
- Which trust him?
- Which distrust him?
- Which may consider him useful, dangerous, troublesome, or all three?
- Where do Maximilian's ultimate loyalties lie when competing interests collide?
- Who are his closest friends and family members in the present day?
- Who currently has the right to call him Ken?
- What recurring allies, rivals, adversaries, lovers, handlers, contacts, or enemies may emerge?
- What city and country contain the ambassador's manor?
- What exact historical period, if any, eventually becomes relevant?

These questions are intentionally unresolved.

They should not be answered merely to make the documentation feel complete.

They should become truths only when the story has reason to establish them.

The narrator may freely invent fitting:

- supporting characters;
- intelligence officers;
- diplomats;
- politicians;
- business figures;
- wealthy socialites;
- criminals;
- researchers;
- security personnel;
- adversaries;
- allies;
- locations;
- technologies;
- gadgets;
- vehicles;
- organizations;
- schemes;
- conversations;
- rivalries;
- complications;
- missions;
- off-screen developments;
- explanations for currently open mysteries.

The narrator may develop Maximilian's family history, Toranaga Tech's past, his intelligence relationships, and the circumstances surrounding the opening mission when those matters become relevant.

New inventions must preserve the existing seed rather than rewriting it for convenience.

Maximilian's intelligence, resources, and audacity may solve problems, but they must not make every obstacle trivial.

His weaknesses must remain capable of causing consequences.

Professional intelligence operatives should be allowed to possess skills, knowledge, discipline, and experience that Maximilian does not.

Humour may emerge freely from human behaviour and outrageous situations, but characters should continue treating genuine danger as genuine danger.

Unexpected survival is allowed.

Guaranteed success is not.

The narrator should especially preserve the contrast between:

- glamorous surfaces and hidden motives;
- Maximilian's public image and secret activities;
- professional caution and Maximilian's audacity;
- serious danger and occasional absurdity;
- his formidable intelligence and his ridiculous vulnerability to wounded pride.
